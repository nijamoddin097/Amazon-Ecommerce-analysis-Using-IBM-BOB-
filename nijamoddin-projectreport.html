5. EDA Q&A Findings (44 Questions Answered)

Pricing & Discount

QWhat is the average product price?
Mean: Rs 13,224  |  Min: Rs 200  |  Max: Rs 79,999
QWhat is the average discount?
29.0% average discount. 68% of orders had a discount greater than 20%.
QWhich category offers the highest average discount?
Clothing (40.0%), followed by Sports (27.5%), Beauty (27.5%), Home (27.5%), Electronics (22.5%).
QWhat is the average final price per category?
Electronics — Rs 32,889 avg  |  Home Rs 7,615  |  Sports Rs 5,627  |  Beauty Rs 1,886  |  Clothing Rs 1,647.

Revenue & Sales

QWhat is the total revenue?
Rs 9.94 Billion total revenue across all orders.
QWhich category generates the most revenue?
Electronics — Rs 6.58B (66%) of total revenue. Home Rs 1.53B, Sports Rs 1.12B, Beauty Rs 376M, Clothing Rs 329M.
QWhich city has the highest total spending?
Delhi (Rs 2.00B) leads, followed by Bangalore (Rs 1.99B), Mumbai (Rs 1.99B), Chennai (Rs 1.98B), Hyderabad (Rs 1.98B).
QWhich payment method is most popular?
All four are near-equal (~25% each): Cash on Delivery (250K), Credit Card (250K), UPI (250K), Debit Card (249K).

Ratings & Reviews

QWhat is the overall average rating?
3.93 / 5.0 overall. Electronics leads at 4.21.
QDo returned items have lower ratings?
Yes — returned items avg 3.86 vs 3.94 for non-returned items.

Returns & Delivery

QWhat is the overall return rate?
11.60%. Beauty (11.76%) and Clothing (11.74%) have the highest return rates.
QWhat is the delivery status breakdown?
Delivered (29.5%), Delayed (29.5%), In Transit (29.4%), Returned (11.6%) — nearly 30% of orders are delayed.
QWhich city has the highest return rate?
Hyderabad (12.23%) and Chennai (12.22%) lead; Delhi (10.60%) and Mumbai (10.74%) are lowest.

Customer Behaviour

QWhich device is most used?
All three nearly equal: Mobile App (33.4%), Web (33.3%), Tablet (33.3%). Web users have highest avg order value (Rs 9,956).
QWhat is the purchase volume by day of week?
Uniform across all days (~142K–144K orders/day). Monday slightly leads (144,470 orders).

Correlations

QIs there a correlation between discount and rating?
Pearson r = -0.069 — weak negative. Higher discounts marginally associated with lower ratings.
QStrongest correlation found?
price ↔ final_price: r = 0.986. All other numeric features are essentially uncorrelated.

Inventory

QHow many low-stock products (stock < 10)?
19,786 records (1.98%). Furniture subcategory has the lowest average stock (248.6 units).

6. Key Business Insights

Electronics Dominance: 20% of order volume but 66% of total revenue — driven by high unit price (avg Rs 32,889).
High Delay Rate: ~30% of all orders are delayed — a critical logistics issue requiring attention.
Discount Trap: Clothing has the highest discounts (40%) yet the lowest revenue — heavy discounting doesn't drive revenue growth.
Payment Parity: All four payment methods share ~25% each — build and maintain support for all equally.
Balanced Geography: Revenue is nearly uniform across all 5 cities (~Rs 2B each) — no single dominant market.
Return Rate: 11.6% overall. Beauty and Clothing most returned — size/fit guides and better product descriptions recommended.
